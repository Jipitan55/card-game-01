🔥 EMBER DUEL

An original card battler. Bring Cinder to 0 life before yours runs out.

🔥Ember — deal damage
🛡️Oak — gain shield that blocks damage
💧Tide — restore life
⚡Spark — damage, and draw a card

Each turn: draw a card, spend 3 energy, then end your turn. Click a card or press 1–7 to play it. Space ends the turn. First to 0 life loses.

🏆 VICTORY!

Cinder's embers fade. The table is yours.

Press R to duel again.

💔 DEFEAT

Cinder's last spark finds you. Shuffle up and go again.

Press R to duel again.
